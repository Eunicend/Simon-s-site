Resources for the hard days

Support is available for grief, recovery, and moments of crisis. You do not have to navigate them alone. You are so so so loved and most definitely not forgotten.

Navigating Grief

Grief is one of the most universal, yet deeply personal, human experiences. There is no single way to grieve, and what you need may change throughout the process. No matter where you are in your grief or what support looks like for you right now, there are resources that may help.

National Grief Resources

A collection of grief support organizations, support groups, educational materials, and resources for adults, children, and families.

Explore National Grief Resources

GriefShare

Find in-person and online grief support groups where you can connect with others navigating the loss of someone they love.

Alcohol & Substance Use Support

Asking for help with alcohol or substance use can be difficult. Recovery does not have to happen alone. These resources can help you or someone you care about find support and treatment.

SAMHSA National Helpline

Free and confidential treatment referral and information for individuals and families facing mental health or substance use concerns.

Call: 1-800-662-HELP (4357)

Visit SAMHSA

FindTreatment.gov

Search confidentially and anonymously for mental health and substance use treatment facilities throughout the United States.

Find Treatment

NIAAA Alcohol Treatment Navigator

A resource from the National Institute on Alcohol Abuse and Alcoholism designed to help individuals and families find evidence-based alcohol treatment.

Explore Treatment Options

Alcoholics Anonymous

Find Alcoholics Anonymous meetings and peer-support resources near you or online.

Find an A.A. Meeting

Emotional & Crisis Support

You do not have to navigate a moment of crisis alone. Immediate, confidential support is available.

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Free and confidential emotional support is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Call or text: 988
