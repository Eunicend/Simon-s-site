            <h1>The Story Behind This Site</h1>

<p>I created this site first and foremost to honor Simon: to keep his memory alive, recognize the impact of a life that ended far too soon, and hopefully allow something meaningful to continue in his name.</p>

<p>On the night of October 2, 2021, Simon, Jack, and Evan were killed in a car crash after Jack's vehicle was struck by a drunk driver traveling at more than 150 miles per hour. They died upon impact. In a matter of moments, three lives were taken and the lives of countless family members, friends, and loved ones were changed forever.</p>

<p>While my personal connection to this tragedy is through Simon, his was not the only life lost that night. Jack and Evan also lost their lives in the crash, and they were deeply loved by families, friends, and communities of their own. Although I cannot tell their stories or speak to who they were in the same way I can for Simon, I want this space to honor and remember them too. Their lives mattered, their absence matters, and the people who continue to carry their loss matter.</p>

<p>I also created this site because I know how much love and care my family and I have received throughout the years as we have learned to navigate and live alongside the reality of losing Simon. I hope that some of that same love and care can be extended to others through this space.</p>

<p>This is a pain I would not wish on my worst enemy. At the same time, I recognize how incredibly blessed I have been to be surrounded by family and friends who have continued to show up for one another throughout the years.</p>

<p>Most importantly, my faith has carried me. I am a follower of Christ, and Jesus is my Lord and Savior. Without God, I genuinely do not know where I would be today, or what my life would've looked like. My faith has not made grief disappear, but it has given me something much greater than my grief to hold onto.</p>

<p>I have come to understand that grief is ongoing. It is something I may carry with me for the rest of my life, but it does not have to overcome me. That does not mean there aren't moments of deep pain, confusion, hurt, numbness, or sadness. It does not mean there haven't been difficult seasons of anxiety, depression, or simply trying to understand how life could continue after the reality I once knew was no longer recognizable.</p>

<p>It means that I refuse to let my story end there.</p>

<p>Grief is not my story. Love is.</p>

<p>As painful as grief can be, I have also come to recognize that there can be beauty within it. Grief is, in so many ways, evidence of how deeply we have loved and continue to love. I have seen the way loss can bring people together, the way people step in to carry one another when the weight becomes too heavy to carry alone, and the way grief can remind us just how fragile and precious this life really is. It has reminded me how important it is to love people fully and love them well while we have the opportunity to do so. To show up for one another. To take care of each other. To say the things that need to be said and make sure the people we love know that they are loved.</p>

<p>That is part of what I hope this site can represent. Simon was, and continues to be, deeply loved. I wanted to create something that honors that love while also extending it outward: to remember him, to give others a place to remember the people they love, and to provide support for those learning to live alongside loss.</p>

<p>In the years since losing him, I have been blessed to experience some of the fullest and most beautiful moments of my life. Those moments do not erase what happened or how deeply I miss him. Instead, they remind me that even alongside tremendous loss, life can still hold love, purpose, laughter, community, faith, and joy.</p>

<p>There is so much pain and suffering in this world. I believe that makes justice, kindness, advocacy for those who cannot advocate for themselves, love, and joy even more important. Those are things I will continue to refuse to let death take away from me.</p>

<p>I hope this site can provide comfort to anyone who has ever had to experience loss. This is for Simon, for Jack, for Evan, for you, for your loved ones, and for every person gone too soon. Every single one of you deserves the love, support, attention, and care you need.</p>

<p>Scripture That Has Carried Me</p>

<p>Romans 8:18 (NIV)
A reminder that the suffering we experience now does not compare with the glory God has promised ahead.</p>

<p>Psalm 63:3–5 (NIV)
A reminder that God's love is better than life itself, and that even in difficult seasons, there is reason to praise Him and find peace, restoration, and healing in His presence.</p>

<p>Psalm 34:18 (NIV)
“The LORD is close to the brokenhearted…” When I didn't know where to turn, God came through just as He said.</p>

<p>These verses have reminded me throughout the years that God does not ask me to pretend that pain does not exist. He meets me within it, and I've experienced it time and time again. My grief and my faith exist together. Their coexistence reminds me that even in the deepest loss, I can still choose to trust, love, hope, and live. And when I couldn't on my own, I didn't have to.</p>
